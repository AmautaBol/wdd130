Our Rafting Trips

Have questions about a specific trip? Contact Us Today!

Half Day Adventure

Perfect for beginners and families. Enjoy a 4-hour guided trip down calm stretches of the river with a few fun Class II rapids. Includes all equipment and a snack.

Full Day Thrill

For those seeking more excitement. An 8-hour journey featuring Class III and IV rapids. We stop halfway for a riverside lunch. Minimum age is 12.

Extreme Rapids

Our most challenging trip. Navigate intense Class V rapids over two days with overnight camping. This trip requires excellent physical fitness and prior experience.
